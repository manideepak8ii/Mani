# Supply Chain & Logistics Analytics

An end-to-end business analytics project focused on operational performance, delivery reliability, supplier and warehouse efficiency, inventory risks, and logistics cost drivers.

## Business questions
- Which carriers, suppliers, and warehouses contribute to delivery delays?
- How do shipping modes and regions affect logistics costs?
- Where do stockouts and supplier lead-time risks appear?
- How do sales, orders, and inventory trends vary over time?
- What operational changes could improve service levels and cost efficiency?

## Analytics workflow
- **SQL (MySQL):** relational schema, data cleaning, KPI analysis, sales, delivery, supplier, warehouse, inventory, logistics, and advanced business queries; uses joins, CTEs, window functions, conditional aggregation, and date functions.
- **Python:** dataset generation, metric calculations, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, and visualizations using Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.
- **Excel:** KPI workbook and reporting with pivots, formulas, and charts.
- **Power BI:** interactive dashboard planning and reporting across executive overview, logistics/delivery, supplier/warehouse performance, and inventory/operations.
- **Business reporting:** methodology, insights, and actionable recommendations.

## Key dataset metrics (as reported in the project analysis)
- 22,000+ supply-chain order records
- INR 1.19B in order revenue
- 77.6% on-time delivery rate (22.4% late)
- 8 warehouses and 10 suppliers
- Air freight identified as a comparatively high-cost shipping mode without an on-time performance advantage
- Warehouse, carrier, supplier, inventory, and regional performance explored
